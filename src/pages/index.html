01 / MONEY MODEL

Stablecoins

Reserve-backed digital tokens

  1. Issuer
  2. Reserve assets
  3. Digital token

A token designed to track a currency’s value. Holding it is generally different from holding a bank deposit; backing and redemption rights depend on its terms.

Explore the model ↗
02 / MONEY MODEL

Tokenized deposits

Bank deposits on programmable rails

  1. Commercial bank
  2. Customer deposit
  3. Programmable record

A bank’s deposit liability recorded on a programmable ledger. In the two-tier model, banks settle with each other in central bank money.
